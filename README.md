# 🥛 Amul Product Sales Performance Analysis (2019–2025)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Amul product sales performance from 2019 to 2025 using descriptive analytics. It explores product-wise sales, annual sales trends, regional demand, revenue, profit, and customer ratings to identify meaningful business insights.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Identify the best-selling Amul products by sales volume.
* Analyze annual sales trends from 2019 to 2025.
* Compare product demand across four regions.
* Identify the highest-revenue products and regional revenue drivers.
* Analyze customer ratings and satisfaction.
* Compare regional retail prices and profit per unit.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Python**
* **Pandas** — Data manipulation and analysis
* **NumPy** — Numerical operations
* **Matplotlib & Seaborn** — Data visualization
* **Jupyter Notebook** — Data analysis

## 📊 Key Findings

* **Top-selling product by volume:** Amul Taaza Milk
* **Highest-revenue product:** Amul Ghee
* **Best-performing year:** 2025
* **Leading region by sales volume:** North India
* **Highest profit per unit:** East India
* **Average customer rating:** Approximately 4.15/5
* **Total revenue:** Approximately ₹57.04 crore
* **Total profit:** Approximately ₹11.36 crore

## 📈 Analysis Performed

1. Product-wise sales analysis
2. Yearly sales trend analysis
3. Regional demand comparison
4. Revenue and profit analysis
5. Customer rating analysis
6. Product–region revenue comparison
7. Business insights and recommendations

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Maintain sufficient stock of high-demand products.
* Focus on Amul Ghee for revenue generation.
* Strengthen distribution in North and West India.
* Monitor customer ratings regularly.
* Review low-volume products to identify growth opportunities.
